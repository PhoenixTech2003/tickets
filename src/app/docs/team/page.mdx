# Scanners & organizers

Scanners check guests in at the door. Organizers help run the event.

## Adding a scanner

People must have **signed in to Tickets at least once** before you can add them.

1. Open the event and go to the **Scanners** tab.
2. Choose **Add a ticket scanner**.
3. Search for the person by name or email.
4. Choose their **Station**:
   - **An entrance:** they're locked to that door. Tickets tied to another door are redirected.
   - **Roaming:** they admit every valid ticket, whatever its entrance. Good for supervisors, or anyone moving between doors.
5. Add them.

<Shot name="scanner-add" alt="The Add a ticket scanner dialog with the person search and station picker" />

They become a **Ticket scanner** automatically. The next time they open Tickets, they go straight to the scanning page with your event ready.

## Managing scanners

<Shot name="scanners" alt="The Scanners tab listing scanners, their stations and their scan counts" />

- **Move a scanner** to another door by changing their station. Their device updates within a second or two.
- **Disable a scanner** with the switch to pause their access. The event disappears from their device until you enable them again.
- **Remove a scanner** to take them off the event. If it was their only event, they go back to being a plain user.

## Organizers

The **Organizers** tab lists everyone who manages the event.

- **Admins** can choose **Invite an organizer**, and can **Revoke** anyone except the owner.
- **Organizers** see the list but can't change it.

An invited organizer can do everything on that event: edit it, manage entrances, tickets and scanners, and watch activity. Only the owner or an admin can delete the event.
