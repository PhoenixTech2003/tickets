# Offline & sync

Tickets keeps a copy of your events on your device, so the dashboard and the scanner keep working without a connection.

## How it works

- When you sign in, your device downloads the events, entrances, tickets and scans you have access to.
- Every screen reads from that local copy, which is why it's fast and updates live.
- Changes from other devices arrive within about a second while you're online.
- **Scans are saved on your device first**, then uploaded. The server re-checks every scan before it counts.

## The sync pill

The coloured pill in the sidebar (dashboard) or the top right (scanner) shows your connection at a glance. Tap it to open the full sync status page.

| Pill | Means |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Live** | Connected and up to date. |
| 🟡 **Syncing** | Sending your scans or receiving changes. |
| 🟡 **Connecting** | Reaching the server. |
| 🔴 **Offline** | No network. You can keep working, and everything catches up when you reconnect. |
| 🔴 **Sync error** | Connected, but something went wrong. Open the sync status page. |

## Scanning offline

<Clip name="scan-offline" alt="Scanning a ticket with the device offline: the Offline banner shows and the ticket is still admitted" />

1. An **Offline** banner appears on the scanning page.
2. Keep scanning as normal. Codes are checked against the tickets on your device.
3. Your scans wait in a queue on the device.
4. When the signal returns, the queue uploads automatically. You don't need to do anything.

<Note tone="warning">
**Don't sign out or clear your browser data while offline.** Unsent scans live on the device until they upload.
</Note>

### When two offline devices admit the same ticket

If two scanners are both offline and the same code is typed on both, both devices may show **Admitted**. When they reconnect, **the first scan to upload wins**. The other is changed to **Already used** and syncs back to that device. Organizers see it in the Activity tab as **device said admitted**.

Keeping devices online wherever possible avoids this.

## The sync status page

Open it by tapping the sync pill, or choose **Sync status** in the dashboard sidebar.

<Shot name="sync-status" alt="The sync status page showing connection state, upload queue and data on this device" />

It shows:

- **Connection status** and when the device last synced
- **Upload queue:** scans waiting to be sent. This should be 0 once you're back online.
- **Current errors**, if any
- **On this device:** how many events, tickets and scans are stored locally. These are what offline scanning checks against.
- **Session log:** connection changes and errors since you opened the app

### Reconnect and Re-download

- **Reconnect** restarts the connection. Try this first if you're stuck on Connecting or Sync error.
- **Re-download** wipes the local copy and downloads it again. Use it if data looks wrong.

<Note tone="warning">
Re-download warns you if the upload queue isn't empty. Clearing would **lose scans that haven't been sent**, so
reconnect and wait for the queue to reach 0 first.
</Note>

## Loading the app with no signal

Once you've opened Tickets on a device, the dashboard and scanning pages load even with no network, after a reload or a restart.
