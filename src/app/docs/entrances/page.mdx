# Entrances

Entrances are the doors guests use. They let you send each ticket holder to the right door and see arrivals per door.

## Do you need entrances?

No. With no entrances, the event is a **single-door event**: every ticket is valid at any door, and every scanner can admit anyone.

Add entrances when you need to:

- keep VIP, staff or general admission guests at separate doors
- see how many guests arrived through each door
- close one door without closing the event

## Adding an entrance

1. Open the event and go to the **Entrances** tab.
2. Choose **Add entrance**.
3. Enter a **Name**, such as *North Gate*. Use exactly what's on your signage. Guests at the wrong door are redirected by this name.
4. Optionally add **Directions**, such as *Past the main car park, left of the stage*. Scanners read these to guests who are at the wrong door.

Each entrance card shows how many **tickets** belong to it, how many **scanners** are stationed there, and how many guests have been **admitted** through it.

## How entrances affect tickets

When you generate a batch of tickets, you can tie it to an entrance. The entrance is printed on each ticket.

| Ticket | Scanner | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Tied to North Gate | at North Gate | Admitted |
| Tied to North Gate | at South Gate | **Wrong entrance:** "Please direct the guest to North Gate" plus your directions |
| Not tied to any entrance | anywhere | Admitted |
| Any | roaming (no entrance) | Admitted, unless the scanner has said which door they're at |

## Closing an entrance

Use the switch on an entrance to close it. Tickets tied to a closed entrance are rejected with **"Entrance closed"**. Everything else keeps working. Switch it back on to reopen.

## Deleting an entrance

Choose **Delete** from the entrance's menu. Its tickets and scanners aren't deleted. They become valid at **any** door. If you want to stop admissions there instead, close the entrance with the switch.
