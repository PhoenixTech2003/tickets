# Tickets & printing

Tickets are generated in batches and printed on A4. Each one carries a unique six-character code that scanners type in at the door.

## Generating tickets

1. Open the event and go to the **Tickets** tab.
2. Choose **Generate tickets**.
3. Set:
   - **Quantity:** 1 to 1,000 tickets per batch.
   - **Entrance** (optional): ties every ticket in the batch to one door. Leave it empty for tickets that work at any door.
   - **Label** (optional): such as *VIP* or *Early bird*. It helps you tell batches apart.
   - **Price per ticket** (optional): printed on each ticket in the event's currency. Leave it empty to print no price.
4. Generate. The batch appears straight away.

<Clip name="generate-tickets" alt="Generating a batch of 50 VIP tickets for the VIP Entrance" />

Tickets are anonymous. They don't carry a guest's name.

### About the codes

- Six characters, always mixing **letters and numbers**, for example `7KQ4MX`.
- Characters that look alike are never used: no 0/O, 1/I/L, 2/Z or 5/S. Codes can be read aloud or typed in a hurry without mistakes.
- Every code is unique across the whole platform.

## Printing

Under **Print batches**, choose **Download every batch**, or use the download button on a single batch. You get a zip file with one PDF per batch.

<Shot name="ticket-pdf" alt="A printed A4 sheet of ten tickets with cut lines" />

- **A4, ten tickets per page** in two columns, with dashed cut lines and scissors marks.
- Each ticket shows **ADMIT ONE**, the event name, date and time, venue, entrance (or *Any entrance*), price if set, the code, and a serial number.
- **Voided tickets are left out** of the PDF.
- Times print in the timezone of the device you download from.

## Finding a ticket

Type a code into **Find a code** on the Tickets tab. Filter by status: **All**, **Unused**, **Admitted** or **Voided**.

<Shot name="tickets" alt="The Tickets tab showing batches, the ticket table and the status filter" />

## Voiding and restoring

Lost or cancelled ticket? Open its **⋯** menu and choose **Void ticket**. A voided ticket is rejected at the door with "Voided ticket". Choose **Restore ticket** to make it valid again.

## Undoing an admission

If a ticket was admitted by mistake, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Undo admission**. The ticket can be scanned again.

## Deleting a batch

Use the delete button on a batch to remove every ticket in it.

<Note tone="warning">Deleting a batch can't be undone, and its printed tickets will stop working.</Note>

## Ticket value

If any batch has a price, the **Overview** tab also shows the total face value of tickets issued and admitted.
