# Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for the problems people run into most.

## Access

### "I signed in but I see You need access"
Your account isn't linked to anything yet. Ask an admin or your event's organizer to add your email, then refresh. See [Signing in & roles](/docs/getting-started).

### "I was added as a scanner but the event isn't there"
- Refresh the page.
- Check the sync pill. If it says **Offline** or **Connecting**, the event downloads once you're connected.
- Ask the organizer to check you aren't **disabled** on the Scanners tab.

### "I can't find someone to add as a scanner"
They need to sign in to Tickets once first. Send them the link, then try the search again.

### "I can't create an event"
Creating events needs the **Event organizer** role, which an admin grants. Being invited to someone else's event isn't enough.

## At the door

### Every scan says "Invalid code"
- Check the **event** in your station picker. You may be scanning against the wrong event.
- Codes never contain 0, O, 1, I, L, 2, Z, 5 or S. If you're typing one of those, it's misread: try the look-alike character.

### Every scan says "Event closed"
The event has been disabled. An organizer needs to choose **Enable event** from the event's **⋯** menu.

### Lots of "Wrong entrance" results
Check your station. You may be set to the wrong door. If you're locked to an entrance, ask an organizer to move you.

### A guest says they haven't been in, but the scan says "Already used"
The card shows when and where the ticket was first admitted. An organizer can search the code on the **Activity** tab to see who scanned it. If it was a mistake, they can choose **Undo admission** on the Tickets tab.

## Sync

### The pill says "Offline" but I have signal
Tap the pill to open sync status and choose **Reconnect**. If that doesn't help, reload the page.

### The pill says "Sync error"
Open sync status to see the error. Try **Reconnect**. If it keeps happening, send a screenshot of the sync status page to your admin.

### Scans are stuck in the upload queue
Stay on the page with a connection. The queue drains automatically. **Don't sign out or use Re-download** until it reaches 0, or the unsent scans will be lost.

### The data looks out of date or wrong
Make sure the upload queue is at 0, then use **Re-download** on the sync status page.

## Printing

### Some tickets are missing from the PDF
Voided tickets are left out on purpose. Restore them on the Tickets tab if they should print.

### The time on the tickets is wrong
Times print in the timezone of the device you download from. Download from a device set to the event's local time.
