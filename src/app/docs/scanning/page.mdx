# Scanning tickets

The scanning page is built for a phone or tablet at the door: type the code, read the result, next guest.

## Before doors open

1. **Sign in once while you have signal.** Your device downloads the tickets for your events. A progress bar shows while this first sync runs.
2. **Check your station** at the top of the page.
   - If an organizer assigned you to an entrance, it's set for you and locked.
   - If you're roaming, or you're an organizer or admin, choose the **event** and optionally the **entrance** you're standing at.
3. **Check the sync pill** in the top right. Green **Live** means you're fully up to date.

<Shot name="scanner-station" alt="The scanning page showing the station picker, code input and sync pill" />

Your device remembers its station, so next time you can start scanning straight away.

## Scanning a ticket

1. Type the six characters from the ticket into **Enter ticket code**. Codes always mix letters and numbers, and letter case doesn't matter.
2. The code is checked **as soon as the sixth character is typed**. There's no button to press.
3. Read the result card. The device vibrates once for admitted and twice for rejected.
4. The input clears and is ready for the next guest. Tap the result card to dismiss it early.

<Clip name="scan-flow" alt="Typing a ticket code and seeing the green Admitted card, then the input clearing for the next guest" />

See [Scan results explained](/docs/scan-results) for what every result means and what to tell the guest.

## Your stats

Under the scanner you'll see:

- **Admitted by you:** how many guests you've let in at this event.
- **Your recent scans:** the last few codes you checked and their results. Use it to double-check a guest who says they were just let in.

## No signal?

Keep scanning. An **Offline** banner appears and codes are checked against the copy on your device. Your scans upload automatically when the signal returns. Read [Offline & sync](/docs/offline-and-sync) for the details.

## Organizers and admins

You can scan too. Choose **Scan tickets** in the dashboard sidebar. Use the dashboard button in the scanner's header to go back.
